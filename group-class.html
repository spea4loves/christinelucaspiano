Group Class

A new year is almost here. It's the perfect time to make your resolution to learn to play music a reality! Beginning in January 2027, I’m starting a group piano class for adult beginners at PYCO Music Center in Wexford, PA. The goal of this class is to have fun and learn how to read and play familiar music in a relaxed, small group, capped at 6 students in a class. The class meets from 11:30 AM-12:30 PM every Monday for 6 weeks, with the option to continue on in subsequent 6-week units. Course cost: $240. Click HERE for more information.
